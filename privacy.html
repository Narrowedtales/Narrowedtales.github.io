NARROWED TALES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy covers Narrowed Tales Uploader, a private tool that the owner of the Narrowed Tales YouTube channel uses to upload the channel's own videos.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the Narrowed Tales channel, set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and add them to the channel's playlists. It only acts on the channel owner's own account.

What we collect

The tool does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about anyone. It does not read viewers' data, comments or analytics. The authorization token that lets the tool upload videos is stored as an encrypted secret in the channel owner's private GitHub repository and is used for nothing else.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact email on the Narrowed Tales YouTube channel's About page.
