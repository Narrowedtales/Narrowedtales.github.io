Narrowed Tales

Original stories, told one chapter at a time. Narrowed Tales is a YouTube channel of original, serialized fiction: narrated stories brought to life with simple line-art animation and atmospheric sound.

Narrowed Tales Uploader

Narrowed Tales Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uploads finished episodes to the Narrowed Tales YouTube channel, along with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists. Every episode is reviewed and approved by the channel owner before it is uploaded.

The tool is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.
